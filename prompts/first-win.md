# Your first win

One real output in your first session. Not a tutorial. One finished thing, in
your voice, trained on your business.

You have already run `make-it-yours.md`, so your agents know your business. Now
you use them. Pick the path that matches what you need today.

---

## Before you start
- Your three context files exist (`context/company.md`, `context/person.md`,
  `context/voice.md`). The make-it-yours prompt wrote them.
- Your `CLAUDE.md` is in place.
- Open the Content Lead (Path A) or the EA (Path B).

---

## Path A: Draft a real post

For when you want something to publish.

1. Open the Content Lead.
2. Paste this:

   ```
   Draft me one short LinkedIn post. Use everything you know about my business
   and my voice from the context files. Topic: the single biggest thing my
   ideal client gets wrong before they come to me. One idea, my voice, ends on
   a clear point. No hashtags.
   ```

3. Read the draft aloud. If a line sounds wrong, tell the agent which one and
   why. It will fix it.
4. One more pass if you need it. Then you have a post you could publish today.

That is the win. Not "I learned how agents work". A real post, in your voice,
that you did not start from a blank page.

---

## Path B: Clean a messy note

For when you want your time back, not a post.

1. Find a messy note: meeting scrawl, a voice-memo transcript, a brain dump.
2. Open the EA.
3. Paste the note, then:

   ```
   Clean this up. Give me the decisions, the actions with owners, and anything
   I need to follow up on. Drop the rest. Keep it short.
   ```

4. You get back a clean, usable note in under a minute. Do it with the worst
   note on your desk and feel the difference.

---

## What just happened

You did not configure a tool. You briefed a team member who already knew your
business and got real work back. That is the whole idea. The blank page is gone.

Do one of these today. Then tomorrow, point your worst weekly time sink at the
EA and see how much of it disappears.
